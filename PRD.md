**1. Product Overview**

**1.1 Product Vision**

Build a premium, technically credible personal portfolio that presents Uttam Singh's academic background, engineering experience, AI/ML projects, technical capabilities, and professional development in a compelling and evidence-backed way.

The portfolio should communicate three things:

1.  **Who I am:** A Computer Science and Engineering student specialising in Data Science, with a strong interest in applied AI/ML and engineering.

2.  **What I can build:** Practical projects involving machine learning, computer vision, signal processing, LLM applications, backend systems, and data-driven solutions.

3.  **How I approach engineering:** Understanding problems, researching solutions, building systems, evaluating results, and continuously improving technical depth.

The experience should combine a conventional professional portfolio with a dedicated analytics experience that allows visitors to explore verified information about projects, skills, experience, and technical interests.

**1.2 Product Objectives**

- Establish a strong professional online identity.

- Position the owner primarily for AI/ML Engineering, Machine Learning Engineering, and Data Science opportunities.

- Make technical projects understandable to both technical and non-technical visitors.

- Demonstrate technical breadth without exaggerating expertise.

- Present career and project information in a structured, visually engaging way.

- Provide an interactive analytics dashboard for deeper exploration.

- Make it easy for recruiters to access the resume, GitHub, LinkedIn, and contact information.

- Provide a maintainable foundation for adding future projects, achievements, publications, and professional experience.

**1.3 Success Criteria**

The product will be considered successful when:

- A recruiter can understand the owner's professional profile within 30 seconds.

- A visitor can reach a project's detailed case study in two interactions or fewer.

- The analytics dashboard clearly communicates what each metric represents.

- All displayed statistics are traceable to actual data or explicitly labelled estimates/self-assessments.

- The site works consistently across desktop, tablet, and mobile.

- The site loads quickly and has no major accessibility or navigation problems.

- Resume and professional links work correctly.

- New projects and experience entries can be added without restructuring the application.

**18. Final Product Definition**

The final product is a premium personal portfolio with two complementary experiences:

**Experience A: Professional Portfolio**

A concise, story-driven presentation of the owner's identity, education, experience, projects, skills, and contact information.

**Experience B: Interactive Analytics**

A deeper, data-driven exploration of the owner's technical work, project composition, professional experience, education, and skill evidence.

The homepage should make visitors interested. The project case studies should establish technical credibility. The analytics dashboard should make the portfolio distinctive and transparent.

The guiding principle is simple:

**Present ambition through direction, competence through evidence, and technical depth through actual work.**

The website should not try to make the owner look like an expert in everything. It should make the owner's real work easy to understand, evaluate, and remember.
